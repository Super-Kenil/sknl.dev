A dead simple base64 encoder and decoder. Drop an image, get a base64 string. Paste a base64 string, get an image. That's the whole thing.

Built this because I kept needing to convert images for inline embedding in emails and documents. The twist? It keeps a history of your conversions, so you don't lose track of what you've encoded before.

Pure HTML and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step. Opens in any browser, works offline once loaded. Sometimes the simplest tools are the most useful ones.
