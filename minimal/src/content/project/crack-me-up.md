You know those moments when webpack is frozen at 99% and you're just... waiting? I made this to fill that void.

Hit a button, get a developer joke. Simple as that. Built with Next.js because I was learning it at the time and wanted something fun to practice with.

The jokes are mostly programming-related puns and one-liners. Some are actually funny, some make you groan. Perfect for those 30-second build pauses that feel like eternity.
