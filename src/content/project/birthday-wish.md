A fun little project for wishing birthdays in style. Enter someone's name, and you get a shareable link with a 3D animated cake just for them.

Built with React and some CSS magic for the 3D cake effect. The link gets auto-copied to clipboard so you can paste it directly into WhatsApp or wherever.

Made this for a friend's birthday and decided to make it reusable. Turns out people love personalized birthday links more than generic WhatsApp stickers.
