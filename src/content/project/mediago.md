A high-performance media converter and editor running client-side. Convert videos and images right in the browser using FFmpeg and WebAssembly, or process folders locally.

Includes an auto background removal tool similar to remove.bg, along with a mask editor to manually erase or touch up parts of any image. Everything runs privately on your device.
